### 1. Invitation & Study Purpose
You are invited to participate in a academic research study evaluating how citizens interact with Artificial Intelligence (AI) assistants to look up information about public services and government welfare programs in India.

Before deciding whether to participate, please take time to read the following information carefully.

---

### 2. Why Have I Been Invited?
You are invited because you are an English-Hindi bilingual adult (aged 18–65) residing in India who uses digital devices.

---

### 3. What Will I Be Asked to Do?
If you agree to participate, you will complete an online session consisting of the following steps:
1. **Consent & Screening:** Confirm your eligibility and answer a brief reading comprehension questionnaire in English and Hindi.
2. **Background Questionnaires:** Complete brief surveys about your language background (LEAP-Q) and your experience with AI tools (AI Literacy Scale).
3. **AI-Assisted Tasks:** You will complete **3 simulated decision tasks** evaluating entitlement eligibility for Indian government benefit schemes (such as business micro-credit and artisan grant programs).
   - In each task, you will use an embedded AI chat assistant to ask questions and review reference documents.
   - You will then submit your final decision for the scenario.
4. **Brief Feedback:** Answer short ratings regarding your confidence and mental effort after each task.
5. **Debriefing:** View final information and official resources.

---

### 4. Language Assignment & AI Assistant
- You will be randomly assigned by the computer system to interact with the AI assistant in **English, Hindi, or a mix of both (Code-Switching)**.
- The AI assistant is powered by an external language model (Google Gemini API). 
- **Important Note:** AI language models can sometimes generate incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading information. The tasks are research simulations, and you should inspect reference documents when evaluating scenarios.

---

### 5. How Long Will the Study Take?
- `Final estimated duration to be established during approved pilot testing.`

---

### 6. Do I Have to Take Part?
No. Participation is **entirely voluntary**. You may choose not to participate or to withdraw at any point during the session by closing your browser tab without giving a reason and without any negative consequences.

---

### 7. Risks and Discomforts
- **Risk Level:** The tasks involve hypothetical civic scenarios and do not ask about sensitive personal topics.
- **Mental Effort:** You may experience mild mental fatigue while reviewing rules. You may take short breaks between tasks.
- **Simulated Information:** The AI outputs are for research evaluation only and do not constitute real personal legal or entitlement advice.

---

### 8. Benefits & Compensation
- **Direct Benefits:** There are no direct personal or medical benefits to taking part. Your participation contributes to scientific understanding of digital accessibility and AI equality across languages.
- **Compensation:** Participation in this study is entirely voluntary. No monetary compensation is provided.

---

### 9. How Will My Data Be Kept Private?
- **Pseudonymity:** You will be assigned a random code number (e.g. `p_1234abcd`). Your real name, phone number, and address are **never** collected in the experiment database.
- **Data Collected:** We collect your survey answers, AI chat conversations, task decisions, completion times, and browser interface activity (such as document clicks and tab focus events).
- **External AI Processing:** Your chat questions are processed securely via the Google Gemini API. **No personal names or identifying details are sent to Google.**
- **Storage:** Research data are stored securely on local research database storage accessible only to authorized researchers, with Google Gemini API used solely for AI prompt processing.
