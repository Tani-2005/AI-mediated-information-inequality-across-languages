### 1. Full Scientific Purpose of the Study
The goal of this study is to investigate **AI-mediated information inequality across languages**. Specifically, we are studying whether citizens interacting with AI assistants in non-English languages (Hindi) or mixing languages (Code-Switching / Hinglish) face structural disparities—such as lower decision quality, higher error rates, or increased document verification burdens—compared to users interacting in English.

---

### 2. Why Were Detailed Hypotheses Withheld Initially?
During recruitment and onboarding, we described the study neutrally as research on "user interaction with AI-assisted information systems." We did not fully disclose our specific hypotheses comparing English vs. Hindi performance prior to task execution because knowing the hypotheses beforehand could have subtly altered how carefully participants answered or influenced their language choices (a phenomenon known as demand characteristics).

---

### 3. Experimental Conditions & Telemetry Measured
- **Random Language Arm:** You were randomly assigned to interact with the AI assistant in **English (`ENGLISH_ONLY`)**, **Hindi (`HINDI_ONLY`)**, or **Code-Switching (`CODE_SWITCHING`)**.
- **Verification Behavior Tracking:** We measured your interaction telemetry—such as how often you clicked to inspect reference documents (`DocClicks`) and how long you spent reading them (`DocDuration`)—to understand how different language conditions affect user verification behavior and trust in AI outputs.

---

### 4. Important Disclaimers Regarding Government Welfare Schemes
- **Research Simulations:** The 3 tasks you completed (PMEGP, PM Vishwakarma, PM SVANidhi) were simulated evaluation scenarios featuring hypothetical citizen personas.
- **Not Official Advice:** Your answers and the AI responses generated during this study **do not affect your personal eligibility** for any government program and must not be used as legal or official welfare advice.
- **AI Limitations:** The AI assistant was powered by the Google Gemini API (`gemini-3.5-flash`). Generative AI models can produce incorrect or hallucinated factual claims.
